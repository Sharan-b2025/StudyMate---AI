Core Engine

Everything a student needs, nothing they don't

StudyMate AI isn't a chatbot with a syllabus bolted on — it's a purpose-built planning system with AI at every decision point.

📄

Upload anything, understand everything

PDF, DOCX, TXT, and scanned images via OCR. StudyMate extracts the text, then rewrites dense material into short, clear notes you'll actually remember.

⏱️

"I have 3 hours" → a real plan

Tell the AI your available time. It ranks topics by importance and progress, then builds a session that actually fits.

📊

Live completion tracking

Every topic is pending, in-progress, or completed. Your syllabus completion % updates in real time on the dashboard.

🧠

Quizzes that hunt your weak spots

Auto-generated from your own material. StudyMate tags every question by topic and tracks which ones keep tripping you up — then feeds that back into your next plan.

📅

Daily & weekly timetables

A full week view of your study sessions, generated and re-optimized as you complete topics.

💬

AI study assistant

Ask questions grounded in your own uploaded material, get study strategy advice, or just stay motivated.
